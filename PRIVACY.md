# Privacy Policy for rclone-personal

Last updated: November 25, 2026

rclone-personal is a personal tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive through rclone.

**Data collected:** None. The app does not collect, store, share, or sell any user data. Files are transferred directly between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own computer.

**Third parties:** No data is shared with third parties.

**Tokens:** Authorization tokens are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration file.
